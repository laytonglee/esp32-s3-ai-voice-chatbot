AI Voice Chatbot

An ESP32-S3 based voice assistant combining audio input, wireless connectivity, AI inference, display output, and voice playback.

📖 Project Overview

A compact AI voice chatbot designed around the ESP32-S3 microcontroller. The system captures voice input through a microphone, processes the audio using the ESP32-S3 and an AI model, and provides the resulting response through visual and audio output. Wi-Fi/BLE provides wireless connectivity for network access and communication.

🧩 Core Components

Hardware & AI components

  • 🎙️ Microphone - MSM261D / INMP441, captures sound and provides audio input to the ESP32-S3
  • 🧠 ESP32-S3 - Central controller that handles audio data, AI model logic, and peripheral control
  • 📡 Wi-Fi / BLE - Wireless connectivity for network access, communication, updates, and data
  • 🤖 AI Model - DeepSeek / Local AI performs inference and processes voice input and responses
  • 🖥️ OLED Display - Displays system status, text responses, or audio waveforms through I²C/SPI
  • 🔊 MAX98357 + Speaker - The amplifier boosts the ESP32-S3 audio signal to drive the 8 Ω speaker
